China in Figures — a national statistics site for China that runs entirely offline in a web browser, on a desktop and on a phone. Six pages reached by links (Overview, Economy, Population, Prices & Living, Regions, Sources) present real 2024 figures from the National Bureau of Statistics of China — GDP of ¥134.91 trillion (+5.0%), a population of 1,408.28 million, consumer prices up 0.2%, and thirteen provinces — each with its source and year, hand-drawn SVG charts, and working controls: a GDP unit switch (¥ trillion / ¥ billion / US$), a population year range, a CPI display switch, and a searchable, sortable provinces table. One self-contained `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the site, its four screenshots and `dossier.json` to `/output`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
